Selected projects

A few projects in more detail. I've left out proprietary and export-controlled specifics.

One notification process for 3,000+ suppliers

Boeing Commercial Airplanes, 2023–2025

Suppliers told Boeing about ongoing quality problems in different ways, which slowed down containment when a defect escaped. I led a 13-person team that put together one notification process, with guidance and tools, for suppliers to follow. It went out to more than 3,000 suppliers and gave the investigation team consistent information to work from.

Composite repair training

Bell Helicopter Textron, 2022

Technicians doing bonding, paint and repair work were falling behind on training, and that showed up as compliance issues. I built a 3D training program on autoclave processing and advanced composite repair to go alongside the existing courses. Retention went up 11% and delinquencies dropped 7%.

Battery life-cycle model

UT Dallas research with Amazon Robotics, 2022

Amazon Robotics needed functional safety requirements for a lithium-ion battery. Alongside the requirements work, I wrote a Python and MATLAB model that estimates the battery's life cycle from test data, and presented the results to their engineers each month.

MRI patient assistance device

UT Southwestern Medical Center, senior design, 2021–2022

Some patients who needed an MRI couldn't use the equipment the facility had. As lead designer on a student team, I worked with a physical therapist and medical faculty to prototype an assistance device using CAD and rapid prototyping.
