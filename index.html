About

I'm an analytical, solutions-driven engineer who has worked in technical problem-solving roles since 2020. Today I'm a Materials, Physics and Process Engineer at Boeing, where I develop and qualify composite materials and processing methods and support composite part fabrication. Before that, I spent two and a half years as a Supplier Quality Engineer leading investigations into supplier defects.

My background spans aerospace and defense: materials and quality roles at Boeing, Leonardo DRS, Bell Helicopter and Collins Aerospace, plus functional safety research with Amazon Robotics at UT Dallas. I hold an M.S. in Systems Engineering and Management and a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering, and a Lean Six Sigma Green Belt.

Outside of work I'm a director with the Society of Hispanic Professional Engineers in Puget Sound, connecting engineers with schools and underrepresented STEM communities. I'm bilingual in English and Spanish, and I recently completed private pilot ground school.
